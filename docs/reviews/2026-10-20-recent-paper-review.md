# Recent arXiv source review: 20 October 2026

Five previously absent papers were identified through bounded title and
abstract searches, then checked against their original Atom bibliography and
selected versioned paper sections. TAG-Nav, already present as bibliography,
received a separate methods and evaluation review. A second AI-assisted source
review checked the compact annotations. This is source review, not experimental reproduction
or a claim that every section of every paper was read.

| Paper | Contribution retained | Comparison boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [GUI world-model decision value](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.24372v1) | Separates prediction from action ranking and studies reference-supervised direct evaluation. | Matched offline ranking and online task success answer different questions; the web evaluator is trained separately. |
| [ECHOES](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.22408v1) | Learns recursive textual memory with privileged history distillation and reinforcement learning. | GUI action/step metrics, diagnostic memory tests and online Sokoban transfer remain distinct. |
| [Source information in Android agents](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.15502v1) | Studies source attribution and two authorization prototypes. | Separate workloads, simulated approval and incomplete trusted browser delivery limit deployment claims. |
| [No-code fix verification](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.11963v1) | Uses GUI executors and issue-specific checks to evaluate proposed browser fixes. | A selected reproducible issue subset, repeated-run aggregation and executor harnesses constrain interpretation. |
| [ORBIT](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.33102v1) | Provides configurable multi-agent evaluations including browser and desktop interfaces. | Adapted scenarios, changing judge/denominator conditions and imported OS-Harm results cannot form one uniform comparison. |
| [TAG-Nav](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.22672v1) | Supplies destination and route memory for Android navigation. | Reaching a reference screen differs from completing downstream operations; graph conditions and app versions matter. |

The five new records retain exact authors, first-submission dates, observed
version dates and abstract fingerprints from their primary API entries.
Version-specific HTML supports the original annotations. No code, dataset,
weight or document license was inferred, and no linked artifact was promoted
as an independently inspected release.

A separately observed DashAct v2 enters the metadata-change queue. Its retained
v1 record and annotation remain intact. A revision is not another paper.

The discovery pass comprised seven bounded query populations, including one
reused feed: 458 overlapping hits reduced to 234 unique arXiv IDs. Fifty-six
were absent from the archive; 17 complete abstracts were read, and five works
were selected for this review. Unread and unrelated hits were not approved.
These numbers describe the queries inspected, not field-wide recall.

The scheduled query now retains its precise title terms and also searches
`GUI` and `Android agent` titles plus four interface-agent abstract phrases.
The 14-day UTC window, 500-result ceiling, daily complete-query reuse and
shared request budget remain in force. Broader retrieval still requires
separate relevance review.
